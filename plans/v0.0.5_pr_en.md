# Move to Qwen3-0.6B and audit training with deterministic inference fixes

## Changes

- Use 28 bound experts; add supervised-token budgets, teacher-free CE training, retokenization, and exposure audits. Fix mixed-precision QKV dispatch.
- Enable deterministic inference by default, add a preallocated native Qwen KV cache, and reduce repeated decoding overhead.
- Run six independent fixed28 recipes covering CE/KL, training duration, data coverage, and learning rate, preserving configurations and diagnostic evidence.

## Validation and results

- Regression suite: 57 passed. All six independent resume/export checks pass.
- Real 0.6B two-GPU FSDP passes packed, compiled, checkpointed KL training, independent resume, and HF export checks.
- Tested untrained eager reference outputs match bitwise. Default SDPA paths differ numerically; all 96 comparisons match bitwise with aligned explicit masks.

| Complete benchmark | Native Qwen3-0.6B | Trained fixed28 candidate | Delta and paired 95% interval |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| MATH-500 | 49.20% | 47.00% | −2.20 pp [−6.00, +1.80] |
| IFEval strict prompt | 57.67% | 49.54% | −8.13 pp [−11.83, −4.62] |

No quality gain is demonstrated. None of the six recipes passes both development gates, so P2 dynamic training and new 32B teacher generation were not started. These results establish no extra-depth or adaptive-exit benefit.

## Limitations and paused work

- The natural data mixture differs from the planned 50/25/15/10 ratio. One training seed; paired intervals do not capture training variance.
- Further work stopped at the user's request. The untrained RMT control preserves 362/500 math responses; IFEval has not started. No partial benchmark score is reported, and inference versus training effects remain incompletely separated.
- Only physical GPUs 4–7 were used. Owned processes are stopped. Recorded stage reservations total approximately 14.16 GPU-hours, including interrupted stages and excluding historical teacher generation.
