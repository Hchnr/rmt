# Audit Qwen3-0.6B RMT training and stabilize deterministic cached inference

## Changes

Move the experiment to Qwen3-0.6B with28 bound experts and an original-order control. Add actual supervised-token budgets, teacher-free CE training, retokenization, exposure audits, and a physical GPU4–7 restriction. Fix mixed-precision QKV dispatch while retaining FSDP, packing, compile, activation checkpointing, and exact resume checks.

Enable deterministic inference by default, use a preallocated native Qwen KV cache, and remove repeated full-history detokenization and set construction during long generation. Add reproducible development probes, controlled training recipes, full evaluation entry points, and paired analysis.

## Validation and findings

- Full regression suite:57 passed. Tested real0.6B original-order logits, cached decode, and HF reload are bitwise identical.
-96 real-model comparisons match HF DynamicCache bitwise with the native capacity cache.
- Six independent fixed28 runs cover CE/KL weights, training duration, data coverage, and learning rate. Exposure checks pass; five runs pass fresh resume and HF export tolerance checks, with the250k independent checks pending.
- Best development candidate: instruction45/48 versus native46/48; math48/48 for both. This narrowly fails the predeclared2pp engineering gate, without establishing statistically significant harm or architectural benefit.
- Per the plan,10M/30M scale-up, main dynamic training, and new32B generation were not executed. Complete formal scores require the audits under reports/v0.0.5; partial runs are not final results.
